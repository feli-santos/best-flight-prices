# Product

**Audience:** travelers who want the cheapest *and* best time to fly. Not power users; simplicity beats features.

## Core journeys
1. **Explore the world** — only an origin is set. Show the 50 most-visited countries (each with up to 3 cities), sorted by cheapest recent fare, with the best months per country.
2. **Plan a place** — a city, country or continent. Show best months with a band (Great/Good/Mixed/Avoid), up to two reasons, the fare, and a suggested stay.
3. **Pick a round trip** — ranked date pairs with price, stops, duration, airline, and an optional provider booking link.

## Inputs
- From: any airport/city (searchable). To: optional (empty = Explore). When: Anytime / Range / Exact. Stay: Auto / N nights (2–21).

## Principles
- Honest data: fares are *cached prices found by travelers recently*, not live. Show their age. Never invent a price, date or link.
- Explainable: bands with reasons, no opaque 0–100 score.
- Simple: 3 screens + a detail sheet, native-feeling, fast, accessible.
- Identical on iOS and Android.

## Non-goals (v1)
Booking/checkout, accounts, push alerts, hotels, multi-passenger pricing, maps, price history charts.
